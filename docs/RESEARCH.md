# Initial Research Context

These sources informed the initial premise. Re-check dates and current guidance before making regulatory or product claims.

## Public frameworks and enforcement

- [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/)
  - Includes third-party AI risks and controls across governance, mapping, and management outcomes.
- [NIST Generative AI Profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf)
  - Discusses procurement due diligence and possible requests for SBOMs, SLAs, and SSAE reports for third-party generative AI.
- [European Commission: General-purpose AI obligations](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/general-purpose-ai-obligations-under-ai-act)
  - Describes technical-documentation and downstream-information obligations for general-purpose AI providers.
- [FTC: Workado AI detection claims](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/04/ftc-order-requires-workado-back-artificial-intelligence-detection-claims)
  - Enforcement example involving allegedly false, misleading, or unsupported AI accuracy claims.
